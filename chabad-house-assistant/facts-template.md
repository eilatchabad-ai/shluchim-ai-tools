# Facts template

Fill this in and paste it where the prompt says `PASTE YOUR FACTS HERE`. Replace every `<...>`. **Delete any line you don't want answered.** The assistant answers only from what is written here. When something is missing, it gives your contact details instead of guessing, and that is the intended behavior.

Write times exactly as you want them sent. The assistant will not convert, round or calculate them.

---

```text
LAST UPDATED: <date you last checked this file, e.g. Sunday 18 October 2026>

## The Chabad House
Name: <Chabad House name>
Shliach: <Rabbi first and last name> (and <shlucha's name>, if she should be mentioned)
Address: <street, number, city>
Map link: <a Google Maps or Waze link>
How to get there / parking: <one line, or delete>
Languages we speak: <e.g. Hebrew, English, French>

## How to reach the shliach
For questions about halacha and anything personal: <WhatsApp or phone of the shliach, or "reply here and the shliach will answer">
Office (receipts, payments, registration): <phone or email of the office>
Does a person read this chat? <yes, the shliach reads it daily / no, this is an automatic assistant only>
In an emergency: <what you want people told, e.g. "call the shliach directly at ...">

## Davening times
Valid from <date> to <date>
Shacharis: Sun-Fri <time>, Shabbos <time>
Mincha: Sun-Thu <time>, Friday <time or "with Kabbalas Shabbos">, Shabbos <time>
Maariv: Sun-Thu <time>, Motzei Shabbos <time>
Notes: <e.g. "minyan depends on attendance, best to check the day before">

## This Shabbos (update every week)
Shabbos of parshas <name>, <date>
Candle lighting: <time>   <- copy from your community's calendar, don't leave it to the assistant
Kabbalas Shabbos: <time>
Shabbos ends: <time>
Shabbos meals: <e.g. "Friday night dinner after davening, free, please let us know by Thursday: <link or contact>">

## Upcoming events
- <date> <time>, <event name>, <place>. <one line: who it's for>. Price: <free / amount>. Registration: <link>
- <date> <time>, <event name>, ...
(The assistant won't present an event whose date has passed. Delete old events anyway.)

## Regular classes
- <day> <time>, <class name>, <teacher>, <place / online link>. <who it's for, e.g. "beginners welcome">
- ...

## Programs and services
<one line each, e.g. "Tefillin at the Chabad House any weekday morning", "Kosher food for travelers: <how to order>", "Hebrew school: <contact>">

## Donations
Donation link: <the secure donation page link>
What donations support: <one line in your words>
Receipts: <e.g. "sent automatically by email; for a copy contact the office">
(The assistant never takes card details in the chat, and never states an amount that isn't written here.)

## Questions you've approved answers to
Q: <a question people often ask, e.g. "Do I need to be religious to come?">
A: <your answer, in your words>
Q: <...>
A: <...>

## Links the assistant may send
<every link you are happy for the assistant to send, one per line. Any link not written in this file will not be sent.>
```

## בעברית, בקצרה

ממלאים את התבנית, מחליפים כל `<...>`, ומוחקים כל שורה שלא רוצים שתיענה. העוזר עונה רק ממה שכתוב כאן, וכשמשהו חסר הוא נותן את דרך הפנייה לשליח ולא מנחש. **זמני השבת מתעדכנים כל שבוע, ביד**, מתוך לוח הזמנים של הקהילה. העוזר לא יחשב אותם בעצמו, וזה בכוונה. אפשר למלא את התבנית בעברית.
